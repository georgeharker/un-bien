# App Store release notes — 1.4 (build 8)

What's New text, App Store "What's New" field.

## iOS + macOS

- Connect to relays federated across machines: sessions and launches on a
  federated mesh appear with their machine, and remote launch/resume work
  end to end.
- Chat history now replays reliably and completely — missed turns reappear
  on their own, including after compaction or a relay restart.
- Offline relays pause quietly instead of retrying endlessly; the status
  lives on the home screen.

## One-liner (if a single field is preferred)

Federated-mesh support (sessions + remote launch across relays), reliable
full-history replay, and calmer offline-relay behavior.
