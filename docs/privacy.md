---
title: "Privacy policy"
---

**Un Bien collects nothing.** The app has no analytics, no telemetry, no
advertising, and no accounts — nothing you do in the app is sent to the
developer, and the app contains no third-party trackers.

- **Your data stays on your devices.** Relay addresses, paired machines, and
  preferences are stored locally on your phone/Mac. Your owner key (the
  cryptographic identity the app generates during onboarding) is stored in
  your device's keychain — optionally synced through your own iCloud Keychain
  if you enable it; that sync is Apple's iCloud Keychain, not a Un Bien
  service.
- **You host the network.** The app connects only to relay servers you
  configure. Conversation content flows between your devices through that
  relay; the relay operator (you, or whoever runs a relay you choose to trust)
  can see routed plaintext. See the
  [trust model](design.md#trust-model-in-one-paragraph).
- **No fixed servers.** Beyond the relays you enter, the app talks to no
  fixed endpoints on the developer's or anyone else's infrastructure.
- **The App Store build is the same build** — the demo mode included for
  review is bundled canned data, not a connection.
